What the evaluation covers

The current results archive contains 303 qualified numerical outcomes from 360 native evaluation slots, with 57 unavailable. Of 24 series, 16 are complete and 8 are unavailable; 32 of 48 questions have numerical outcomes and 16 are unavailable. It also records 443 of 445 core fits and 164 of 165 contrasts.

These results describe the configured components and their evaluation conditions. They do not establish a general method ranking or calibrated error control. The evaluated DIABLO, fuseMLR and IntegratedLearner configurations do not reproduce every part of the packages’ high-level workflows.

The current protocol is a post-execution consolidation, not a preregistration. Earlier records retain their stated prospective or historical roles.

Reproduce a run

Each ordinary run records its resolved configuration, input identity, split information, diagnostics, model outputs and runtime. Retain these with the inputs when inspecting an analysis.

omicau verify --config config.json --audit run/audit.json

The archived evaluation scripts are separate from ordinary command-line and browser runs. Use the environment pins and numbered reproduction phases supplied with the evaluation archive to inspect the study.
