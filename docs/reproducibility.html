What the evaluation covers

The evaluation combines controlled simulations with five retrospective molecular applications. Each part addresses a different question.

EvaluationRecorded coverageInterpretation
Simulation bank737 completed datasets out of 740 intendedThree infrastructure-unavailable cases are retained without replacement.
Preprocessing-fault experiment20 matched leakage pairsCompares training-only preprocessing with deliberately global preprocessing.
Molecular-background challenge40 conditional draws on one fixed backgroundAssesses absent versus planted candidate signal, without independent-cohort replication.
Installed-package applicationsFive random-forest applicationsUCEC and BRCA histology, KIRC stage, LGG IDH status, and DepMap PRMT5 dependency.
Core comparative extension443 of 445 fits; 164 of 165 contrastsIncludes Block Forest comparisons, feature-cap sensitivity and alternative partitions; 42 model summaries are complete and one is unavailable.
Expanded native panel303 qualified outer slots out of 360; 16 complete series out of 24Only complete 15-slot series enter comparisons: 32 numerical and 16 unavailable contrasts against the random-forest and Block Forest baselines.

Complete expanded-panel series cover the four classification cohorts for dense multiblock PLS-DA in the DIABLO framework, Cooperative Learning, Priority-Lasso and the configured fuseMLR component. No IntegratedLearner series or expanded-panel DepMap series met the complete-series requirement. The archive retains all 57 unavailable slots and their convergence or input-eligibility reasons; qualified slots from incomplete series are not pooled.

The comparisons describe the evaluated configurations. DIABLO uses dense multiblock PLS-DA without sparse feature selection; fuseMLR uses its inverse-loss weighted-mean component with cross-fitted random-forest bases; IntegratedLearner uses native BART and aggregation components under controlled cross-fitting. Block Forest uses bounded external tuning. Priority-Lasso retains an outer-training representation within its native lambda cross-validation, as documented in the protocol. These are not full high-level workflow matches or evidence of a general method ranking.

Expanded-panel comparisons are secondary and descriptive. Their intervals resample biological groups, keeping repeat predictions together, and condition on the fitted predictions. They do not establish retraining uncertainty or simultaneous coverage. The 120 privileged-representation recovery fits and 80 generating-equation diagnostics explain the synthetic mechanisms; they are not deployable predictors or new estimates of power.

The current protocol is a post-execution consolidation, not a preregistration. Earlier records retain their stated prospective or historical roles.

Reproduce a run

Each ordinary run records its resolved configuration, input identity, split information, diagnostics, model outputs and runtime. Retain these with the inputs when inspecting an analysis. The following command recomputes the aligned-input fingerprint and compares it with the audit; it does not replay model fitting or check every configuration or environment setting.

omicau verify --config config.json --audit run/audit.json

The archived evaluation scripts are separate from ordinary command-line and browser runs. Start with the archive README and protocol/EXECUTED_STUDY.md, then follow the environment pins and numbered reproduction phases. Earlier protocol documents retain their freeze-time wording; the executed-study record and numbered deviations explain their final scope.

The public archive supplies aggregate results, scientific scripts and source-access instructions. Molecular matrices, individual outcomes, identifiers and prediction vectors are not included. Reproducing prediction-level checks requires obtaining the documented public sources under their access terms and generating those records locally. The omicau bootstrap --dataset mock example exercises the general workflow without molecular-data access.

The archived reproduction record documents selected script replays and installed-package application runs. It does not claim a second end-to-end fit of the full simulation bank or a fresh download of every public dataset.
