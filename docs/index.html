How to read the comparison

Removing assay B leaves assay A. The difference is the full-model score minus the score after omitting B and refitting. In the report, an increased-performance designation requires the family decision interval to lie entirely above zero.

Here the scores and interval bounds are illustrative values. In an analysis, intervals summarize saved held-out predictions and exclude variability from retraining or selecting another partition.
